SDM is an independent radio specializing in experimental and heavy music. Initially, SDM focused on black metal bands, and noise bands. Later, the radio expanded its list by adding experimental electronic music groups, deathcore, metalcore, sludge, as well as stoner rock bands. SDM has its own publishing house called SDM_01 Publishing.

S—Dynamic Music

Our music label specializes in publishing and promoting metal music. We strive to support talented artists who create quality and original music in this genre. Our goal is to provide musicians with a platform to spread their creativity and help them succeed in the music industry.

1.0

We are actively looking for new talented artists who can contribute to the development of the metal scene. Our label is open to musicians of all metal genres from classic heavy metal to more modern genres such as black metal, death metal. We hold auditions and select the most promising artists to offer them a collaboration.

Once we find the right artist, we start collaborating with them. Our team of professionals helps musicians in recording, mixing and mastering their tracks, as well as in creating high-quality music videos. We also organize concerts and tours for our artists so that they can present their music to a wider audience.

One of the key aspects of our work is promotion and marketing. We actively use social media, online platforms and other channels to promote our artists. We create advertising campaigns, organize press releases and cooperate with other music labels and distributors. Our goal is not only to help our artists achieve success, but also to contribute to the development and growth of the metal scene as a whole. We support young musicians, give them the opportunity to express themselves and create quality music. We also participate in organizing festivals, concerts and other events dedicated to metal culture.

In general, our music label aims to become a reliable partner for talented metal performers, helping them to realize their potential and achieve success in the music world.
