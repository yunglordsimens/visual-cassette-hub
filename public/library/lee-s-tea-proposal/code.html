With your own website

You have something nobody else has

In central Prague, nobody offers real Vietnamese phin-dripped coffee, egg coffee (Cà Phê Trứng), or such a collection of mochi and Asian desserts.

On Wolt, nobody sees this. It's hidden among hundreds of items. Your own website puts it front and center.

☕ Cà Phê Trứng — Egg Coffee

Famous Hanoi specialty. Creamy, sweet, unforgettable. This is your main trump card. On the website it will be the star — with story, photos, and atmosphere. This creates content people want to share on Instagram and TikTok.

Brand Identity & Logo Update

To make the website and your physical store truly stand out, we should consider how the brand is presented.

A Custom Typographic Logo

Currently, your logo uses a complex graphic. For a modern, premium look, I suggest moving towards a text-based logo (wordmark).


As I study Typography at UMPRUM, I can design a custom, unique font specifically for Lee's Tea. This will capture the authentic Vietnamese street culture and modern vibe without needing complex illustrations, making it perfect for cups, stickers, and signage.
